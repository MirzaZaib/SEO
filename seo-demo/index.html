SEO Demo Lab

This section of the Digital Art Studio site exists purely to teach SEO. It contains four paired example pages — a "poor practice" version and a "best practice" version — for both on-page SEO and off-page SEO. Open each pair side by side, view page source, and compare.

On-Page SEO Examples

On-page SEO covers everything you control directly in a page's HTML and content: titles, meta tags, headings, alt text, structured data, internal linking and content quality.

Poor Example

Poor On-Page SEO

Missing meta description, no viewport tag, multiple/misused headings, no alt text, keyword stuffing, no structured data.

Best-Practice Example

Best On-Page SEO

Optimised title/meta, semantic HTML5, proper heading hierarchy, descriptive alt text, JSON-LD schema, internal links.

Off-Page SEO Examples

Off-page SEO covers signals that happen outside your own page: backlinks, citations, social proof, and brand mentions. A single static page can't fake real backlinks, so these pages demonstrate the on-page signals that support (or sabotage) off-page authority.

Poor Example

Poor Off-Page SEO

Orphaned page with no internal links pointing to it, no social profiles, no citations, no shareable content, no schema "sameAs".

Best-Practice Example

Best Off-Page SEO

Consistent NAP data, linked social profiles, review/testimonial schema, share buttons, and a documented link-building strategy.

Quick Comparison

FactorPoor PracticeBest Practice
Title tagGeneric, no keyword, or duplicated across pagesUnique, keyword-focused, under ~60 characters
Meta descriptionMissing or auto-truncatedUnique, compelling, ~150–160 characters
HeadingsMultiple H1s, skipped levels, no keyword relevanceOne H1, logical H2/H3 hierarchy
ImagesNo alt text, oversized unoptimised filesDescriptive alt text, sized and lazy-loaded
Structured dataNoneJSON-LD for Organization/Product/Breadcrumbs
Internal linkingOrphan pages, no contextual linksPillar/cluster linking with descriptive anchor text
Backlinks & citationsNone, or spammy/irrelevant link farmsRelevant citations, consistent NAP, earned mentions
Social signalsNo profiles, no share optionsLinked profiles, share buttons, social proof
