Our Link-Building Strategy (Summary)

  • Guest illustrations and interviews on relevant art and design blogs, with a natural backlink to our services.
  • Listings in legitimate local business and creative-industry directories with identical NAP details.
  • Digital PR: press coverage when we complete notable commissions or community art projects.
  • Encouraging satisfied clients to leave reviews on third-party review platforms.

Why This Page Has Good Off-Page SEO

  • Internally linked from the main navigation, so it receives normal link equity (unlike the orphaned poor example).
  • "sameAs" structured data connects the business to verified social profiles.
  • Review and AggregateRating schema can produce rich snippets and build trust signals.
  • Consistent NAP citations across external platforms reduce confusion for search engines.
  • Share buttons make it easy for real visitors to amplify content off-site.
  • A documented, ethical link-building strategy instead of spammy tactics.
