Why This Page Has Good On-Page SEO

  • Descriptive, keyword-focused <title> under ~60 characters, unique to this page.
  • Written meta description (~150–160 characters) that summarises the page and encourages clicks.
  • <html lang="en"> and a viewport meta tag for accessibility and mobile-friendliness.
  • One <h1> followed by a logical <h2>/<h3> hierarchy — no skipped levels.
  • Descriptive alt text on every image, plus explicit width/height to avoid layout shift.
  • Semantic HTML5 (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <section>, <article>, <footer>) instead of table-based layout.
  • Descriptive internal link text ("Digital Art Prints" rather than "click here").
  • Canonical tag, Open Graph tags and JSON-LD structured data (WebPage + BreadcrumbList) for richer search and social results.
  • Original, human-written content with the target keyword used naturally, not stuffed.
