About Digital Art Studio

Digital Art Studio is a (fictional) collective of digital artists and illustrators founded in 2018. We help individuals and brands turn ideas into original digital art — from personal portrait commissions to full illustration campaigns.

Note for students: This entire website, including this business, is an educational example built to demonstrate real-world on-page and off-page SEO techniques. It is not a real company.

Our Values

Originality

Every artwork is created from scratch — we never resell templates or stock illustrations.

Collaboration

We work closely with clients through every revision to make sure the final piece matches their vision.

Craftsmanship

Our artists specialise in digital painting, vector illustration, and portrait work using industry-standard tools.

Meet the Studio

Our small team of illustrators and digital painters has completed commissions for hundreds of clients, ranging from independent authors needing book covers to small businesses needing brand illustrations.
