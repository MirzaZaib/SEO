Digital Art Trends to Watch in 2026

Digital art continues to evolve quickly. Here are the trends our artists are seeing across client commissions this year.

1. Maximalist Color Palettes

Bold, saturated color combinations are replacing the muted palettes popular a few years ago — especially in brand illustration work.

2. Portrait Commissions as Gifts

More clients are requesting custom portrait commissions as personalised gifts rather than traditional photography.

3. Prints as Home Decor Statements

Large-format digital art prints are increasingly used as centerpiece decor rather than smaller accent pieces.

4. Collectible Digital Art

Interest in curated digital collectibles continues, with buyers looking for original artwork prepared for an NFT digital art marketplace.
